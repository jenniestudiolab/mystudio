# Workbook: Discovering Your Big Five Personality Traits

Welcome to your Big Five Personality Traits self-assessment workbook! Understanding your personality is the first step toward building productivity systems, time management strategies, and leadership styles that work for you.

For each of the five traits, you’ll answer 10 real-world questions and score yourself from 1 to 10, where:

**1** means *This rarely applies to me*\
**10** means *This describes me perfectly*

After each section, your average is calculated automatically. Ready? Let’s get started!
